voicewho is speaking

origin is the built-in synthesizer: instant and the most alien, but the least real. The others are recorded human voices reading the creature's sounds. Each was trained on a different human language, so each gives a different accent. clarity, gravity, patience, grace and haste are quick: a few seconds for a paragraph (63 MB each). The ones marked + sound the most lifelike and are slow: they take about as long to make as the speech lasts (one 92 MB download shared by all six). Each downloads once, then works offline. how many sounds does a language need?

Each distinct sound is a phoneme. English has about 24 consonants and 20 vowel sounds. The smallest known language, Rotokas, works with 11. Most use 20 to 40. Fewer sounds just means longer words.
